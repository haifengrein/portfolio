Many friends ask me, with AI writing code, are programmers still valuable?

My answer is: **Absolutely.**

### 1. Code is Poetry of Logic

Programming is not just about tasks; it's about structuring chaotic thoughts. Every function and component is a crystallization of thinking.

### 2. Extension of Creativity

AI is the tool, humans are the architects. The stronger the tools, the bigger the blueprints we can realize.
