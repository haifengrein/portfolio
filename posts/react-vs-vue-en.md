This is an eternal topic. React is flexible, Vue is elegant. The choice depends on your team DNA.

- React gives you architectural freedom, which also means you must create your own guardrails.
- Vue ships with sensible defaults and is great when you need quick, consistent delivery.

**My take:**

1. Pick React for mid-to-large projects when you have senior frontend architects.
2. Pick Vue for smaller teams that want fast onboarding and strong defaults.
